## 👋 About Me

Hi! I'm Hanke Xie (解晗轲), a second-year master's student at [Northwestern Polytechnical University](https://www.nwpu.edu.cn/) (NPU). I am a member of the [Audio, Speech and Language Processing Group (ASLP@NPU)](http://www.nwpu-aslp.org/), advised by Prof. [Lei Xie](http://www.nwpu-aslp.org/lxie/). I received my Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the School of Computer Science at NPU in 2025.

My research focuses on speech generation and spoken interaction, including text-to-speech, singing voice synthesis, and LLM-based spoken dialogue systems. My recent work spans zero-shot TTS, long-form conversational and podcast speech generation, low-latency streaming TTS, and continuous-latent speech generation.
